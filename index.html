GENETICS GARDEN

GOAL

Breed every look of all six species: pure a, pure b and the mixed hybrid c.

HOW TO PLAY

  1. Walk around and click any creature to read its gene card.
  2. Get close and click to pick it up. You have two hands.
  3. Carry two of the same species into the fenced breeding ground.
  4. Click that species' stone, stand inside, then click the glowing orb.
  5. A baby is born! Flowers sprout somewhere in the garden, animals appear by the gate.

GENES

Every creature has two genes. a is dominant: a pure pair (aa) shows the a look. b is recessive: it shows only as a pure pair (bb). One of each (ab) is a hybrid with the mixed look c. Hybrids can breed again.
